What is Flashing?

Flashing = installing new software on your eko. Think of it like updating your phone's OS, but for microcontrollers. One click. No tools. No coding.

With eko dev lab, just plug in via USB and hit flash. We handle the rest through Web Serial — right in your browser. It's that easy.
