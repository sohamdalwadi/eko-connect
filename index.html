What is Flashing?

Flashing is the process of writing software (firmware) directly to the memory of your eko device. It's like installing an OS on your phone or computer, but specialized for microcontrollers.

With eko dev lab, you don't need any complex tools. Just connect your device via USB and click flash. We use Web Serial to handle the communication securely right in your browser.
